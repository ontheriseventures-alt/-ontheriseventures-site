About Us

On The Rise Ventures, Inc.

A Central & West Coast Florida holding company behind a small group of independently run businesses across the region, led by a love of aviation.

Who We Are

One company, several ventures

On The Rise Ventures was founded by Michael Scola, CPA, a Certified Public Accountant turned aviation entrepreneur. Michael holds Commercial and Private Pilot certificates from the FAA and a Florida real estate license, and has been directly involved in aircraft acquisitions and sales for years.

That same hands-on, entrepreneurial approach carries through every business under the On The Rise Ventures name: aviation, aircraft sales, Cirrus pilot services, watches, construction, handyman services, and business consulting, each serving clients across Central & West Coast Florida.

Every business under On The Rise Ventures is run directly and reachable through the same team. Call or email us and we'll connect you with the right part of the business.
