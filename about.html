On The Rise Ventures, Inc.

A Marco Island, Florida holding company leading with aviation consulting and parts sales, and backing a small group of independently run businesses across Southwest Florida and the Tampa Bay area.

Who We Are

One company, several ventures

On The Rise Ventures is based in Marco Island, FL. Our flagship business is aviation consulting and parts sales, supporting aircraft owners, operators, and maintenance teams. Alongside it, we back independently run businesses in aircraft sales & leasing, watches, construction, handyman services, notary work, and business consulting — each serving clients across Marco Island, Naples, and the greater Tampa Bay area.

Every business under On The Rise Ventures is run directly and reachable through the same team — call or email us and we'll connect you with the right part of the business.
