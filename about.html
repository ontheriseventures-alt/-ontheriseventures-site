About Us

On The Rise Ventures, Inc.

A Central & West Coast Florida holding company leading with aviation consulting and aircraft parts sourcing, and backing a small group of independently run businesses across the region.

Who We Are

One company, several ventures

On The Rise Ventures was founded by Michael Scola, CPA — a Certified Public Accountant turned aviation entrepreneur. Michael holds Commercial and Private Pilot certificates from the FAA and a Florida real estate license, and has been directly involved in aircraft acquisitions and sales for years, with 10 aircraft transactions completed in the past year alone.

That same hands-on, entrepreneurial approach carries through every business under the On The Rise Ventures name — aviation consulting, aircraft parts sourcing, aircraft sales, watches, construction, handyman services, and business consulting — each serving clients across Central & West Coast Florida.

Every business under On The Rise Ventures is run directly and reachable through the same team — call or email us and we'll connect you with the right part of the business.
