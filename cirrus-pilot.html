What We Do

Cirrus-specific contract flying

Whether you want a captain in the right seat for a trip, a safety pilot to keep you current, or your airplane flown somewhere while you're not available, we fly SR20s and SR22s specifically, and know the airplane inside and out.

Captain Services

Need a qualified captain for a trip, a business meeting, or a family flight? We'll fly your Cirrus for you, start to finish.

Safety Pilot

Working on instrument currency or proficiency? We'll fly right seat so you can log the approaches and stay sharp.

Ferry Flights

Just bought or sold a Cirrus, or need it repositioned for maintenance, an annual, or a pre-buy? We'll fly it there.

Dealer & Broker Support

Reliable pilot coverage for dealers and brokers moving inventory, demo aircraft, or trade-ins.

Why Cirrus Owners Choose Us
