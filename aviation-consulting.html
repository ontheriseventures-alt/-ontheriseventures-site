What We Do

Straight answers on the numbers behind owning and operating aircraft

We work with aircraft owners and operators on the financial and operational decisions that come with ownership — from day-to-day cost management to buying or selling an aircraft.

P&L Analysis

Reviewing operating costs, revenue streams, and profitability drivers to see where an aircraft or flight operation actually stands financially.

Operator Model Analysis

Evaluating ownership and operating structures — cost-per-hour modeling, utilization, and whether the current setup fits how the aircraft is actually used.

Acquisition & Sale Consulting

Advising buyers and sellers through the financial side of a deal — valuation, deal structure, and whether a transaction makes sense.

Operator Project Management & Maintenance Coordination

Coordinating maintenance projects and vendors on an operator's behalf, and managing the moving pieces so owners don't have to.
