What This Is

A friendly sounding board, not a paid consultancy

This isn't a formal, fee-based consulting practice — it's free, informal advice from someone who enjoys talking through the numbers behind owning and operating aircraft. If you have a question about costs, a potential deal, or how your operation stacks up, feel free to reach out. No engagement, no invoice.

This is offered independently and informally, at no cost. It is not affiliated with, and should not be taken as advice on behalf of, any employer or aviation company.

Talking Through P&L Questions

Happy to look over operating costs, revenue streams, and profitability drivers and share a second opinion on where things stand financially.

Operator Setup Chats

Casual conversations about ownership and operating structures — cost-per-hour, utilization, and whether the current setup fits how the aircraft is actually used.

Buying or Selling? Let's Talk

If you're weighing a purchase or sale, happy to talk through valuation and deal structure informally, as a friend in the industry — not as a broker or paid advisor.

Maintenance & Project Questions

General thoughts on coordinating maintenance projects and vendors, based on experience — not a substitute for your own project manager or A&P.
